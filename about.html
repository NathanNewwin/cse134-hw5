Technical Interests

I am interested in building useful programs that solve practical problems and make information easier to work with. That interest naturally extends into web development and plugins, where I enjoy creating applications that are clear, reliable, and easy to maintain.

I am also developing a deeper focus on machine learning, homelabs, server networking, and cybersecurity. Machine learning lets me combine mathematical reasoning, data analysis, and software implementation in systems that can identify patterns at scale. With homelabs and server networking, I enjoy working closer to the infrastructure layer by configuring machines, understanding how services communicate, and learning how networks stay reliable. Cybersecurity ties these areas together by requiring technical decisions to account for trust, risk, and the protection of users from the beginning.
